# Information-preserving VCG views

All 164 patients, 391 recordings and 55,863 original beats retained. Raw and denoised sources have matching hashes, labels, row order, XYZ leads and original crop dimensions. Every template is the median of one recording's own denoised beats, never another patient's beats or a label-conditioned dictionary. All beats of a held-out recording are available for its own unlabeled median: this is offline within-exam context, not streaming inference.

Five views are raw, denoised, record median, denoised-minus-median and raw-minus-denoised. Both residual reconstructions pass 1e-6 absolute/relative tolerance. These deterministic views create no new independent patients or guaranteed diagnostic information. Residuals are not labeled noise. No R redetection, temporal shift, spatial rotation, time warping, quality filtering, per-beat amplitude normalization, delineation or relabeling is used. All view/lead scaling is deferred to training partitions. Original waveforms are unchanged; views are assembled lazily to avoid duplicating gigabytes. This stage is per-beat classification with record context; it does not train a chronological sequence encoder or Tucker factor along beat order.
