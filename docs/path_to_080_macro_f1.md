## Where the error budget goes

Replayed the two verified out-of-fold prediction banks over all 164 patients, ten folds and three seeds. Source probability and membership hashes and all ensemble metrics reproduced. The following analysis uses fixed completed predictions.

The two models both miss 36 patients, including 7 of 17 anterior and 8 of 16 anterolateral cases. A retrospective label-aware selector can choose one of the two saved top labels for each patient. Its maximum is 0.736055 macro F1 and 128/164 accuracy. To reproduce the macro F1 calculation, use the three-seed mean probability argmax for each model. Choose the true class wherever either model offers it, which increases the true class F1 and cannot decrease the displaced class F1. For the 21 shared errors with the same prediction, the choice is fixed. Enumerate the 32,768 assignments across the 15 shared errors with different predictions and maximize the mean of the six class F1 values. This diagnostic is specific to the two saved top labels. It consumes held-out labels solely for analysis and supplies no fitted decision rule. [Verified source comparison and memberships](../results/model_error_comparison_v1/REPORT.md)

Setting just the current anterior and anterolateral class F1 values to 1.0 while holding the other four constant yields a hypothetical mean of 0.795903. Reaching the target calls for improvements across multiple MI subtypes. [Current class metrics](../results/r_relative_convolution_v1/complete/REPORT.md)

## Work on two timescales

1. **Use a subtype specialist on the existing cohort.** Fit a binary MI/HC classifier and a five-class conditional subtype classifier on the exact same cached convolution features and training folds. For each subtype, multiply the conditional probability by the estimated MI probability. Include all six classes in inference and evaluation. Compare with a flat multinomial classifier using identical feature inputs and inner tuning budget. Report patient macro F1, class F1, and the three error counts above. This is a controlled test of whether the decision rule can reduce within-MI errors. [Backlog item 12](RESEARCH_BACKLOG.md#12-probabilistic-detection-and-localization-readout) · [Related inter-patient detection and localization study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38599223/)
2. **Audit the labels and signals before selecting another representation.** Use a reviewer blinded to model outputs, with a mix of common errors and class-matched correctly classified patients. Compare documented region, multi-territory evidence, ECG timing and recording quality. Retain original PTB labels, membership and folds. Findings guide hypotheses and future label collection. [Current error diagnostics](../results/model_error_comparison_v1/REPORT.md)
3. **Add measured XYZ patient data with independently supported region labels.** Request de-identified waveform data from a partner. Prefer cardiac MRI or independent clinical adjudication for infarct location and extent, and seek coverage across all six labels. Split patient development and locked external evaluation before model or label-map selection. Use new development patients to test whether richer regional labels help through an auxiliary region-prediction head. Use the fixed six-class loss alongside this head and mask labels where corresponding regional evidence is unavailable. In a learning curve, hold the validation population fixed while increasing the number of patient training examples. The earlier curve rose from 0.472 to 0.550 mean member macro F1 from about 113 to 148 training patients. It supports testing scale and cannot forecast the patient count required for 0.80. [Learning curve](../results/neural_learning_curve_v1/complete/REPORT.md) · [Partner cohort protocol](measured_vcg_partner_cohort.md)
4. **Check a published orthogonal ECG archive as an additional lead.** The CSE diagnostic study recorded standard ECG and orthogonal XYZ simultaneously in 1,220 adults and used independent clinical evidence. The published categories include broad anterior, inferior and combined infarction. Confirm waveform and label access, electrode geometry and usable role before any alignment or transfer experiment. Broad regional supervision can be tested as a separate source task. [Primary CSE study](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJM199112193252503)

The target is 0.80–0.90 on the defined endpoint. Use the same patients, labels, six classes and evaluation metric for comparisons inside the PTB development track. A paper reporting a higher score supports a numerical benchmark only when its modality, prediction unit, classes, split and metric match. For example, Li et al. report PTB inter-patient localization accuracy of 67.17%, whereas this project uses patient macro F1 on measured XYZ. Confirmation of any major gain requires a separately acquired evaluation cohort and a frozen model. [Primary inter-patient study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38599223/)
